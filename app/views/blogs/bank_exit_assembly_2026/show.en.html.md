## Two days to meet, exchange, and build together

The Bank-Exit Assembly is a gathering for those who want to collectively reflect on our relationship with banks, money, and financial institutions — and, above all, on ways to regain control over our economic lives.

This second edition will be an opportunity to **meet in person**, share our experiences, continue the discussions and reflections that began during the first Assembly, and develop new avenues for action.

Because alternatives cannot be built by each of us alone, we need to meet, compare our experiences, and pool our ideas.

## A weekend in Brittany 🌿

The Assembly will take place at a venue located **near Redon, in Ille-et-Vilaine (35)**.
